What I built to
Navigation
Frosted pill dock. Profile · Home · Browse · Party. Home is across services; Browse is the current service’s WebView. Every tab shows its name. Inbox lives in the Home header.
Service switch
Tap the provider name in the Home header (icon + name + ▾, like Slack’s workspace switcher) to open Switch app. On Browse, tap a service logo in the pill above the dock. Switching during a live video confirms first. The Party tab is how you return to a live watch.
New user
YouTube open: trending only for YouTube. If every service needs login: dashed Connect a service card fills Home. Now-on / Apps still open the grid; logos jump into that service’s sign-in.
Premium theme
Backdrop + CTAs: purple magenta for free, gold for premium — same assets as Android tp_gradient_bg / ic_premium_bg.
Manage accounts
Connected rows use a chevron. Sign out appears once in a confirm sheet after you open options.
Form factors
Phone · glass dock. Foldable · more 16:9 cards, same 168×94 tiles. iPad · nav rail, two-column Home. iPhone Duo · Home left, Party/Apps right across the hinge. Never size a tile as a % of the display.
Title tap
One sheet everywhere: Start a party / Watch alone, handing off by content ID.
Trending tabs
Selecting a service slides the chips so that tab sits on the left — same motion as Apple’s category pills. No notification bell on Home.
Astryx look
Optional visual pass on gothic tokens. Same IA as Option D — not a second product.
