Should we use this?

What Astryx actually is

A React 19 + StyleX design system (beta, v0.6). Gothic is dark-only: ink body #101314, ice text #E8F1F6, muted success/error, 12px container radius. It is guidance, not a lock-in. It cannot ship as a package in Compose or SwiftUI — we would port tokens and rules, not components.

Why I’m unsure

Raw gothic looks like a Meta admin surface, not Teleparty. The accent is ice-white, not magenta. Type (Fustat) and desaturated greens/reds will feel foreign next to our posters. Use the Gothic ice toggle in the playground to see that. Magenta is an overlay so the product still reads as us.

What changed vs Option D
No FAB
Five equal tabs, Slack-style: Home · Party · Apps · Inbox · You. Browse is no longer a center button. You pick a service in Apps, then watch from Home / Party.
Home
Quiet header, small Now on chip (not a fat provider pill), inbox icon with badge. Party card is one container — poster, huddle, then your cam/mic. Jump back + trending stay, without nested cards.
Inbox
Header icon is the glance shortcut (badge). Inbox tab is the list: invites, someone started watching, expired sessions, quota. Same destination either way.
Liquid glass dock
Floating bar, 12px inset, blur + hairline + inner highlight. Active tab is a concentric inner pill — no colored glow (Astryx forbids glow as elevation).
Provider switcher
The old wallpaper sheet is gone. Apps is an Apple TV / Smart TV grid: now-on hero, recently used, all apps, add. The Home chip opens the same grid as a sheet. Switching during a live video confirms first.
Party card AV
Each avatar shows cam or muted-mic. Summary line: “3 cameras · 2 mics live”. Your Cam on / Mic on pills toggle on the card, Discord-huddle style.

Interactive

How to read this

Portable bits

4px spacing, concentric radii (dock 22 / tab 16), one card not a card-in-a-card, quiet shadows, semantic success/error instead of neon green/red. Those can land in Compose without Astryx.

Don’t take as-is

Fustat, ice accent, and the gray-green status colors. If we keep Astryx we’d theme it (they expect that) — gothic is a starting kit, not the Teleparty look.

Apps vs FAB

Slack puts the workspace switcher in the rail; Apple TV puts apps on a home grid. We combined those: Apps tab is the switcher, Home stays “what’s happening in the party.”

Option D still ships

Nothing on this page replaces Home. Use this to decide nav + switcher + inbox + AV status, then we fold the winners back into D / Compose.
