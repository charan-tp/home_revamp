How Home holds together

Premium = gold theme, not pink on gold

Toggle Premium · gold bg in the playground. Backdrop uses ic_premium_bg; CTAs shift gold. Free keeps magenta.

Provider switcher, not a Home title

Tap the header to open the service grid. Columns follow the sheet width — three on a phone, more on Foldable and iPad. Filter with All, Free, and Premium. A gold star marks premium. The current service has the pink ring.

New user, and New user v1

New user uses the same provider header as No party yet — icon, name, chevron — and skips Continue watching. Trending and Switch to Premium stay. The connect card is not on Home. New user v1 titles the page Teleparty, also skips Continue watching, and that connect card lives centered on Browse.

Browse is the service WebView

No Teleparty hub, search, inbox, or Return card over the WebView. Home’s provider name (with ▾) still opens the service grid. The Party tab is how you go back to a live watch.

Trending tabs

Selecting a service slides the chip row so that tab sits on the left, like Apple’s category pills. Home has no bell — Inbox is not on this screen.

Paywall, not a toast

Crunchyroll / Paramount as a free user opens the Premium sheet. Quota exhausted uses “No free parties left this week.”

Tiles never stretch

Phone, Foldable, iPad, and iPhone Duo keep 16:9 title cards. Extra width adds more cards — never a percentage of the window.
