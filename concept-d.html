How Home holds together

Premium = gold theme, not pink on gold

Toggle Premium · gold bg in the playground. Backdrop uses ic_premium_bg; CTAs shift gold. Free keeps magenta.

Provider switcher, not a Home title

Home’s header is the current service — icon, name, dropdown — not a “Home” title. Full brand colour stays on the service’s own WebView chrome.

New user: connect card, not locked trending

We don’t scrape catalogs you aren’t signed into. Empty Home uses the dashed Connect a service card. Trending appears after a service is linked (YouTube counts when it’s open).

Browse is the service WebView

No Teleparty hub, search, inbox, or Return card over the WebView. Switch from the service logos above the dock. Home’s provider name (with ▾) still opens the sheet. The Party tab is how you go back to a live watch.

Trending tabs

Selecting a service slides the chip row so that tab sits on the left, like Apple’s category pills. Home has no bell — Inbox is not on this screen.

Paywall, not a toast

Crunchyroll / Paramount as a free user opens the Premium sheet. Quota exhausted uses “No free parties left this week.”

Tiles never stretch

Phone, Foldable, iPad, and iPhone Duo keep 16:9 title cards. Extra width adds more cards — never a percentage of the window.
