How Home holds together

Premium = gold theme, not pink on gold

Toggle Premium · gold bg in the playground. Backdrop uses ic_premium_bg; CTAs / FAB shift gold. Free keeps magenta.

White chrome marks

Pill, FAB, and Browse chrome use white mono service marks. Full brand colour stays on content.

New user: connect card, not locked trending

We don’t scrape catalogs you aren’t signed into. Empty Home uses the dashed Connect a service card. Trending appears after a service is linked (YouTube counts when it’s open).

Browse chrome is not a browser

No URL field. Back + service identity + overflow. Switching is only via the Switch Services sheet.

Browse with no accounts

Matches production Hub Home: copy plus Add My Accounts — not a fake catalog. Home still uses the connect card.

Paywall, not a toast

Crunchyroll / Paramount as a free user opens the Premium sheet. Quota exhausted uses “No free parties left this week.”

Tiles never stretch

Phone, Foldable, iPad, and iPhone Duo keep 112×168 posters. Extra width adds columns or a second pane — never a percentage of the window.
