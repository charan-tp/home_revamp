What changes vs today

Same architecture

ExoPlayer keeps the 16:9 slot. Chat + invite / cam / mic / GIF / reactions stay under it.

Idle overlay

Replaces the flat pink banner + white button with Home CTA language (primary pill, free/premium gradient).

Live state

Player shows title, provider, progress — then chat. Browse is how you pick; Party is where it plays.

Guest · not signed in

If the party’s service isn’t linked, the player shows Not Signed In and a Sign in button. Chat stays.

Host vs guest

Guests wait for the host to pick. Title sheets say only the host can change the video. Members list + invite link (not a code).

Loading / disconnected / switch / unsupported

Buffering overlay, Retry vs Ok, service-switch warning, and Video Unavailable for services not on mobile.

People bar

Thin strip under the player: who’s here, Invite, Leave — without eating the player.
