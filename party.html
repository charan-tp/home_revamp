What changes vs today

Nothing on the picture

No floating faces. Telegram / WhatsApp keep people in a row; YouTube keeps chat under the video. We do both.

People bar

Avatars + cam/mic live under the player. Tap someone for the members list. Speaking gets a green ring.

Chat like Telegram

Name in color, message after it. Composer is GIF + field + send — cam/mic are not in the text field.

Idle overlay

Replaces the flat pink banner + white button with Home CTA language (primary pill, free/premium gradient).

Guest · not signed in

If the party’s service isn’t linked, the stage shows Not Signed In and a Sign in button. Chat stays.

Host vs guest

Guests wait for the host to pick. Title sheets say only the host can change the video. Members list + invite link (not a code).

Loading / disconnected / switch / unsupported

Buffering overlay, Retry vs Ok, service-switch warning, and Video Unavailable for services not on mobile.
