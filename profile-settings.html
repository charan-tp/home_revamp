What changed vs today’s screenshots

Profile ≠ Settings

Matches Home · Party · Apps · You. You owns the person; Settings owns the knobs from You. Manage my accounts stays one tap from You.

Tier accents only

Free = purple bg + magenta edit ring. Premium = gold bg + gold badge / avatar ring / toggle on-state. Sign Out stays a danger gradient (not primary gold).

Subscription placement

Upgrade / Manage lives on Profile next to the badge — not buried under Support. Restore stays for non-active subs.

Still the same content

Video compatibility, notifications, Contact Us, Discord, User ID copy, Additional info → What’s New, QA (debug), Sign Out → Get Started.
